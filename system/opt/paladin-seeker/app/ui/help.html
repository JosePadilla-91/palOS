Seeker Help

Seeker

Seeker draws your desktop and the menu bar, and opens folders in windows. Click Seeker in the dock for a new window. The menu bar always shows the menus of the application you are using.

Views

Use the view buttons in the toolbar, or the View menu: icons, list, columns and Carousel. In Carousel, scroll or use the arrow keys to flip through your files.

Keyboard shortcuts

Tips

Press the Space bar to Quick Look the selected items. Hold a file over a folder while dragging and the folder springs open. Hold Ctrl or Alt while dropping to copy instead of move. Drag a folder onto empty space in the sidebar to keep it there.
