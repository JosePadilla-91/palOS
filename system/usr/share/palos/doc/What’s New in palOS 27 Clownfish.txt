palOS 27 Clownfish

Firefox
  Firefox is palOS's web browser. It keeps your profile, bookmarks and tabs from one launch to the next,
  closes cleanly (no more "quit unexpectedly"), uses palOS's look and Open/Save sheets, and speaks the
  language you chose for palOS.

NVIDIA graphics
  palOS installs NVIDIA's own driver for GeForce RTX 20 series and newer graphics chips, straight from
  NVIDIA. Older NVIDIA chips use palOS's built-in graphics driver.

Sound
  Sound goes where you'd expect from the very first second: headphones and speakers you plug in come first,
  the computer's own sound next, a screen's sound last. You can always choose another output in the menu bar
  or in Settings.

Copying files
  Dropping files into a folder copies them once, without asking to replace the files it just copied.

Languages
  palOS speaks 16 languages besides English, including Ecclesiastical Latin.

The Library
  Fonts, Icons, Sounds and Themes show palOS's own.
